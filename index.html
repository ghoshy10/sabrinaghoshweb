About

Taught by a scientist
who does the work

Most tutors teach biology from a textbook. I teach it from a research lab. As a Cambridge PhD candidate actively publishing in neuroscience, I bring the kind of depth and real-world scientific thinking that no amount of exam revision can replicate.

I work with ambitious, driven students at US private schools who want more than a grade — they want to understand science at a level that sets them apart in college applications, AP exams, and future research.

Having studied and worked at some of the world's most competitive institutions, I know what it takes to succeed in rigorous scientific environments — and I bring that knowledge directly to my students.
